# Calm Editorial Story Pages

## Goal
Refine every individual story page with the selected Elegant Editorial Flow direction while preserving the approved Hattiesburg Hub identity, page content, photo credits, galleries, related stories, navigation, themes, SEO, and sharing behavior.

## Changes
- Keep the full-width lead photo, but soften its overlay and transition into the article header.
- Give the headline a calmer scale and line height, with more space around labels, title, author, and date.
- Narrow the main reading column and increase paragraph line height and vertical spacing for a steadier reading rhythm.
- Restyle photo captions, credits, gallery spacing, and section dividers with quieter hierarchy.
- Add more breathing room before “More Original Reads” and “Related Stories” without altering their cards or links.
- Tune mobile spacing and headline sizing so long titles remain comfortable and never overflow.

## Brand and Functionality Guardrails
- Retain Space Grotesk headings, Inter body copy, semantic theme colors, and automatic light/dark themes.
- Do not change story data, routing, SEO metadata, article images, social previews, gallery controls, or content order.
- Preserve existing accessible labels, keyboard behavior, and reduced-motion support.

## Validation
- Check a long story with a single image and a story with a gallery on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm image loading, article metadata, gallery controls, related-story links, and both themes remain intact.
- Confirm the preview builds without errors.
