# Refined Editorial Refresh

Apply the selected **Refined Editorial Layout** direction as a light, site-wide polish. Preserve all existing content, sections, links, newsletter behavior, story ordering, themes, logos, and footer credit.

## What will change

- Slim the floating navigation, tighten its spacing, and simplify its border, shadow, and active-link treatment.
- Reduce the homepage’s oversized feel with a shorter lead image, calmer headline scale, tighter spacing, and smaller action controls.
- Replace heavy rounded surfaces and pronounced shadows with modest radii, fine borders, and restrained elevation.
- Standardize section headings into a clean editorial pattern with compact labels, subtle rules, and consistent links.
- Refine story cards with slightly tighter image proportions, clearer metadata, gentler motion, and more consistent spacing.
- Lighten the featured story, newsletter callout, ticker, events, and footer while preserving their content and purpose.
- Keep the current navy and electric-blue palette, Space Grotesk headings, Inter body text, glass treatment, and automatic light/dark theme.
- Preserve responsive behavior and ensure the smoother density carries through tablet and mobile views.

## Technical details

- Update existing homepage presentation components and shared style tokens only.
- Use semantic theme colors throughout, with one shared easing and restrained shadows.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and retain visible keyboard focus states.
- Remove the unused starter stylesheet if it is not imported.
- Verify the refreshed homepage at desktop and mobile sizes and confirm the preview builds cleanly.
